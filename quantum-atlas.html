Playable website · Front-end development · February 2025

The Quantum Atlas

A visual, playable introduction to quantum computing made by a three-person team. I worked with a physics graduate student to understand the subject and with an illustrator to shape the game flow, then turned the illustrator’s static images and layers into the public website.

Play the live website ↗ View code on GitHub ↗
My contribution

Co-designed the game flow and built the front-end experience in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, including responsive layouts and interactions.

Team of three

The illustrator created the visual assets and collaborated on the flow. The physics graduate student brought subject expertise. I translated the assets and flow into the working website.

From handoff to launch

The source material arrived as static images and separate layers. I assembled them into an interactive site that visitors can play online.
