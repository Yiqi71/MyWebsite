AI interviewer in VR

I am conducting a mixed-methods study of how people respond to an AI interviewer in VR during emotionally sensitive life-story interviews.

Research overview

Comparing AI-led and human-led sensitive interviews.

The study included 58 participants. Each completed two AI-led interview segments and one human-led segment, along with pre- and post-session questionnaires, in-depth interviews, and eye tracking.

I conducted open coding of 22 hour-long interview recordings. Analysis is ongoing as we synthesize the qualitative themes with questionnaire and gaze data.

Preliminary findings are informing design considerations for making AI interviewers more trustworthy and emotionally appropriate in sensitive conversations.
