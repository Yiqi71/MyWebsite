Launched website · UX design & engineering

Dunes Dictionary

A bilingual dictionary built for finding a term and following the relationships around it. I helped shape the UI/UX with two teammates, then independently built the public website and a separate admin site for usage monitoring and content updates.

Visit live website ↗ Public website code ↗
My role

UI/UX collaboration; sole technical implementation of the public website and analytics dashboard.

Team

Six people. Three of us contributed to UI/UX, including me; another teammate led visual identity, and other teammates led content planning.

Context

Developed with a cultural community that uses social media content to democratize knowledge and make specialized ideas easier to understand. Featured in Next Culture Producer, Season 4: Text, a Chanel Culture Fund and Power Station of Art initiative.

01 / The interaction challenge

A dictionary for lookup and exploration.

A conventional list makes it easy to retrieve one entry, but it hides the relationships among concepts. This project needed both a direct route to a known term and a way to move through connected ideas. During research, I studied physical information archives and digital references such as Wikipedia to think about how discovery and navigation could coexist.

02 / Three ways in

One map, three entry points.

Visitors can arrive at a concept through a search, a related entry, or the map itself. Each path leads back to the same connected dictionary.

01 / Search

Find a known term.

Search in Chinese or English, choose a result, and let the map move to its entry.

02 / Connections

Follow an idea.

Open a related term from an entry and continue reading without returning to the search results.

03 / Map

Browse what catches your eye.

Move through the visual map, choose a concept, and open its entry to learn more.

A technical decision

Keep the map in place while zooming.

Challenge

Zooming a large map can move the area a visitor wants to explore out of view.

Approach

I recalculated the map offset around the cursor or pinch center as the scale changed.

Result

The chosen area stays in view during mouse or touch zoom.
